THE MARCH TO IMPACT
WHICH MARCH MADNESS TEAMS ACTUALLY MOVE THE WAGE NEEDLE?
Forget seeds — these are the 68 tournament teams ranked by how much they actually raise graduates' earnings above what a high school diploma would have gotten them, adjusted for regional cost of living, graduation rates, and school accessibility. Spoiler: of 68 teams leave students worse off than if they'd never enrolled.

IMPACT — Headline score: earning power × grad rate × accessibility × scale. Higher = lifts more people more.
ADJ ROI — Lifetime return vs. not attending. Dropouts get zero benefit but still paid tuition.
COL GAIN — Annual raise over a HS worker in the same region, in real purchasing power.
GRAD% — 6-yr graduation rate. Under 50% = most leave with debt, no credential.
ACC% — Acceptance rate. Higher = more accessible.   SIZE — Enrollment (K).
RPP — Regional Price Parity (100=nat'l avg). <100 = cheaper. >100 = pricier.
DRAW — N=National B=Broad R=Regional.   COST — Net price after aid.
T — S=Elite A=Strong B=Solid C=Even D=Neg F=Deep Neg
■ Green=best ■ Blue=good ■ Gray=avg ■ Orange=caution ■ Red=bad
1. HS baseline: $50K/yr (BLS 2025) scaled by state RPP. Iowa: $43.9K. NY: $53.1K.
2. Draw: National (≤25% → nat'l baseline), Broad (26-60% → blend), Regional (>60% → state).
3. Real Earnings = Nominal × (100/RPP). $58K in Iowa = $66K purchasing power.
4. Annual Gain = Real Earnings − Blended Baseline. Can be negative.
5. AdjROI = GradRate×(Gain×40 − Cost×4.5) + (1−GradRate)×(−Cost×2)
6. Impact = AdjROI × (AccRate/50) × log₂(Enroll/5K)
Sources: BEA RPPs 2024 · Georgetown CEW 2025 · College Scorecard · BLS CPS Q1 2025 · IPEDS · CDS 2024-25
